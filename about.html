Our story

Built on road works, grown into supply

Epomeno Investment Company Limited is a limited liability company registered with the Uganda Registrar of Companies. More than half of its life so far has been spent in engineering and supplies, with experience in road construction and in the supply of road furniture.

Our key staff come from reputable engineering firms. Bringing that workforce together under one name is the reason the company exists: clients get a crew that can lay a culvert, and a procurement desk that can deliver the compactor, the cement and the office laptops in the same week.

Today we work as a subcontractor to international main contractors on Kampala and Mukono road programmes, and as a direct supplier to consultancies, institutions and businesses.
