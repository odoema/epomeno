Terms clients can plan around

Our standard proforma terms. Project contracts follow the main contract's conditions.

  1. Quote

    Itemised proforma with quantities, brands and 18% VAT shown.

  2. LPO

    Your local purchase order confirms the items and delivery point.

  3. Delivery

    Within three working days of the LPO for stocked items, with a signed delivery note.

  4. Payment

    By bank transfer within five working days of delivery.
